WHY KINK KORPS EXISTS

No velvet-rope morality.

Kink Korps is for people who want the room a little louder, stranger, sexier, darker, and more alive.

We built it for the people who never quite fit the polished version of nightlife. Queer, trans, straight, gay, bi, exhibitionist, private, experienced, brand-new, dressed to kill, barely dressed at all, or just curious. There is room here.

Music matters. Atmosphere matters. The people in the room matter. The goal is a night that feels free, welcoming, and genuinely fun.

THE VIBE

Come ready to have a night.

We keep the social contract simple so the rest of the night can be fun.

01

03

Keep it fun.

No pressure, no pestering, no unnecessary drama. If something feels off, grab staff.
