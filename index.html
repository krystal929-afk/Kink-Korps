WHY KINK KORPS EXISTS

No velvet-rope morality.

Too many spaces call themselves open-minded right up until somebody's version of kink makes them uncomfortable. Kink Korps exists because we wanted something better.

Adults should be able to show up queer, trans, straight, gay, bi, exhibitionist, private, experienced, brand-new, dressed to kill, barely dressed at all, or simply curious without being treated like there is one correct way to belong in the room.

The line is not whether somebody's kink looks like yours. The line is consent, boundaries, respect, and behavior.

THE ENERGY

Dark room. Loud music. Good people.

HOUSE RULES

Freedom needs a spine.

Anything goes does not mean anything goes without consent.

01

Consent is mandatory.

Ask first. Get an enthusiastic yes. Respect the answer immediately.

02

No means no.

No arguing, negotiating, following, pestering, or trying to wear somebody down.

03

No kink shaming.

You do not have to be into it. You do have to treat other adults with respect.

04

Respect privacy.

Do not treat somebody else's night, body, scene, or identity as content without permission.

05

Look out for each other.

If something feels wrong, get staff. Community works when people actually act like one.

06

21+ only.

Bring valid ID. Event-specific venue rules will always be listed with the party announcement.
